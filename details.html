FAQs

Are my kids allowed to come?

If they're over 16, yes! If not, please reach out to us, and we'll figure out some other options.

Can I bring a plus one?

No. Seating is limited, so we're being super intentional with everyone who's attending.

What should I wear?

Picture black tie, high fashion! Elegant, but also colorful. We encourage you to dress up and to also dress in your culture or ethnicity. We'll absolutely love it!

No sneakers, no jeans, no casual wear. You will not be let in.

I'm traveling into town! Where should I stay?

We're putting together hotel options and fun things to do before and after the wedding. Check out the Travel section for updates!

Is there a wedding registry?

Yes! We'd really love contributions to our honeymoon, but if you'd rather not, we'll also have a list of things we need. Head to the Registry section for details.

Is the wedding outdoors or indoors?

The ceremony and cocktail hour will be outdoors, and the reception will be indoors!

Are pictures allowed during the wedding or reception?

No phones during the ceremony! We want everyone to enjoy the moment. During the reception and everything else, go crazy!
